ROGUE DRAUGHTS

a run-based checkers gauntlet — win, shop, survive the boss
THE RUN. Up to 9 rounds of draughts. First to 5 round-wins takes the match. Every 3rd round is a BOSS with a rule twist. Losing a round never knocks you out — you just earn less gold for the shop.

DRAUGHTS. Men move diagonally forward. Jumps need a space to land in, and they chain — jump, land, jump again. Two enemies standing directly adjacent can never be vaulted in one leap. Toggle whether captures are forced on the menu; in P2P the host's setting applies. Reach the far row to crown a king. No pieces or no moves = round lost. After 100 plies, material decides.

THE SHOP. Spend gold between rounds on charms (passives), board upgrades (extra men, kings, shields) and one-shot actions (retreat, swap, smite). Tiles spawn on your half each round: gold pays out, spring lets a man leap like a king, warp pairs teleport, traps destroy the first enemy to land, forts make your piece unjumpable, and the volcanist erupts neutral lava for everyone. Upgrades persist for the whole run, Balatro-style — stack them and late rounds get gloriously chaotic.

P2P. Host shares the 4-letter code; the joiner enters it. Pure peer-to-peer via PeerJS.
