GEARWORKS

A drafting table for gear trains — build, mesh, and run whole mechanisms.

Building

  • G Gear tool — click empty space to plant a new axle with a gear. Click an existing axle to stack a second gear on it (a compound gear — the heart of any real ratio train).
  • Drag a gear near another gear on the same layer and it snaps to perfect meshing distance. Meshed teeth align and turn together when you run.
  • B Beam tool — click two axles to link them with a rigid beam (structure / visual frame).
  • M Motor tool — click an axle to attach a motor. Set RPM and direction in the panel. Multiple motors are allowed.
  • Layers: gears only mesh with gears on the same layer. Use layers to route trains past each other in 2.5D, exactly like stacked plates in a real machine.

Running

  • Space or ▶ RUN simulates the whole mechanism with exact tooth ratios. Every driven axle shows live RPM.
  • Red gears are jammed: either two gears overlap too deep, or a loop demands two different speeds of the same axle (a real gridlock — fix the tooth counts).

Designing a slot machine

  • Three big reel gears (e.g. 60T) on layer 0, spaced apart.
  • One motor axle with a small pinion; use compound pairs (big gear + small gear on one axle) to step the ratio down so reels spin at different speeds — that's what desynchronizes them.
  • Beams form the chassis plate holding all axles.
  • Odd/even gear counts in a chain flip direction — chain an idler gear in when a reel must spin the other way.

3D view

Switch with the 2D / 3D buttons or Tab. The 3D view is a true WebGL render with a depth buffer, so meshing teeth, stacked compounds, and frames draw correctly from every angle. Every tool works in 3D: click the ground to plant gears, click axles to stack compounds, drag parts (mesh-snapping included), run beams, attach motors, delete. Navigation: left click selects and highlights (hovering highlights too, and dragging a part moves it), middle-drag pans, scroll zooms, right-drag orbits. Hold Shift and left-drag on empty space for rubber-band multi-select.

Beams are Technic-style structure: each beam is horizontal at a chosen layer (set in the panel), riding in the gap just below that layer\u2019s gears — between the gear planes, so a beam can never pass through a gear mesh. Stack beams on different layers to frame the full 3D structure your gears mount in.
